# Non-stationary campaigns: where reactivity has a structural reason to pay. Plan v2 (2026-10-31)

Status: v2 after an external review (gaps G1-G8, decisions in section 8). Nothing built or run. Every closed line (AF choice, surrogate, region, fall-back, source re-weighting) was a stationary campaign, where a fixed good setting is hard to beat. Here the objective changes mid-campaign, so no fixed strategy is right for the whole run. A typed model only counts if it beats a numeric controller on the same state.

## 1. Question
When the objective changes during a campaign: (a) how much does knowing *when* it changed save (headroom), (b) can a numeric detector recover that from the campaign's own observations at an acceptable false-alarm cost, (c) does a soft response (stale data as a down-weighted source) beat a hard restart, and (d) only if a decision headroom exists that numeric rules cannot reach: does a typed model add anything?

## 2. Simulator (real tabular landscapes)
- Pair tasks A and B from the same exact grid (LCBench: 30 tasks; TabRepo: per model family). Both tasks are excluded from the source set of any arm that uses sources.
- Total budget as usual (2D+1 initial + min(50, 25 % pool) steps). At step T_c the objective switches from y_A to y_post. Observations before T_c are from y_A and stay in the data unless the controller discards them.
- **y_post = (1 - a) y_A + a y_B**, a in {1 (abrupt), 0.5 (partial)}. T_c at 25 % or 50 % of the campaign, never revealed to controllers.
- **Regret and normalisation are defined on y_post** (its own max and median on the grid), pre-computed per pair and a. For a = 1 this is y_B. (Review G2.)
- Pairs: *related* and *unrelated* by pre-declared full-grid Spearman tertiles (top and bottom), seeded draw, three disjoint sets: pilot (T1), detector tuning, test. Caveat: full-grid Spearman is a global property; detectability depends on agreement near the optimum.
- After a restart, previously evaluated configs may be evaluated again and return y_post; this paired signal is a free transfer channel (test in T0).
- **Stationary control runs** (no change) share every controller to measure false-alarm cost.
- Metrics: post-change AUC of normalised regret (primary), pre-change AUC, **detection delay** (steps from T_c) and **regret accrued within the delay window**, to split a gain into detection speed vs response quality.

## 3. Controllers
| Arm | Definition |
|---|---|
| oblivious | standard BO keeping all data |
| **oracle-time** | knows T_c only; discards stale data; restarts with a generic design (n0 random points). The deployable ceiling and the denominator for N1 |
| oracle-target | knows T_c and B's identity; restarts from the W1 portfolio built for B. True upper bound, reported, not gated |
| oracle-keep | knows T_c; keeps stale data with a fixed per-point noise variance (below) |
| window | sliding window of the last w = 15 observations, no detection |
| periodic-restart | restarts every 25 % of the post-n0 budget (timer control; coincides with T_c only by chance) |
| CUSUM | tracks standardised GP prediction error of each new observation; on trigger applies restart (generic design, as oracle-time) |
| random-trigger | fires at a random step at the detector's overall rate |
| shuffled-state detector | CUSUM fed prediction errors permuted over steps |
| **SP-stale** | after the trigger (and, as a variant, continuously) the stale observations enter as one extra source row, a GP fitted to them and predicted over the grid, next to the real source tasks; weights as in SP (kappa 10, lambda .25, c .25, frozen from `PLAN_source_prior_lcbench.md`). New extension of `meta/sp.py`, tested in T0 |

Pre-declared mechanisms (review G7): oracle-keep and window-free arms give stale points fixed observation variance 0.1 (standardised units) and fresh points 1e-4; sensitivity at 0.03 and 0.3. Restart design = n0 points; sensitivity n0/2. These values are my choice; they are fixed before any data.

## 4. Gates (pre-register before data)
Regime-conditional (review G4). **Gated regime a priori: unrelated x abrupt** (a = 1). Other regimes (related, partial) are robustness, reported but not gated. N1 is evaluated on the regimes where N0 passed in the pilot (T1, disjoint pairs).
- **TH-N0 (headroom):** oracle-time at least **10 %** below oblivious in post-change AUC on the gated regime (ratio of means over pairs, Wilcoxon p < 0.05, both families). Also report all regimes and oracle-target. If N0 fails in the gated regime, close.
- **FA budget (review G5):** measured in T1 on stationary controls as cost per false alarm c_FA (AUC increase of a campaign with one forced restart). Allowed false-alarm rate = min(10 %, 0.02 / c_FA). If that is below 2 %, the detector is declared infeasible and the line stops with that finding.
- **TH-N1 (numeric detector):** on the N0-passing regimes, CUSUM at least **5 %** below oblivious post-change AUC, no more than **2 %** worse than oblivious on stationary controls at the FA budget, at least 2 % better than random-trigger, shuffled-state and periodic-restart. Report share of oracle-time gain recovered and delay.
- **TH-N2 (soft vs hard):** SP-stale versus the best of {oracle-keep, oracle-time restart, CUSUM restart}: reported; pass if at least 2 % better than CUSUM with p < 0.05 on the N0-passing regimes. Honest prior: SP did not beat W1 in stationary runs, so this is an open comparison, not an expected win.
- **TH-N3 (typed model), conditional (review G8):** T3 runs only if, in T2, the response-oracle (best of {continue, discount, restart} per pair) is at least **5 %** better than the best single fixed response *and* the best response varies with a feature a numeric rule cannot trivially compute. Otherwise T3 is struck. If it runs, the state summary is pre-declared in a separate plan and must include semantic content (hyperparameter names, per-source agreement, proposal locations) beyond the error stream; the bar is the tuned numeric response rule on the same state, plus shuffled-state and state-blind controls.
- Both summaries reported; ratio of means primary. Pilot verdict is per regime, not aggregate.

## 5. Stages (stop and ask at each)
- T0: `meta/nonstat.py` (separate from `meta/bo.py`) and the SP-stale extension. Tests: change applied at T_c exactly; oblivious equals the stationary engine when no change; regret and normalisation use y_post (a = 0.5 case checked against a brute-force max); oracle-time discards exactly the pre-change observations and uses a generic design with no source information; oracle-target uses B's portfolio; a pre-change config re-evaluated after restart returns y_post and the pool index handles it; periodic and random triggers deterministic; SP-stale excludes A and B from sources; determinism; stationary runs unchanged. Pair lists frozen. No compute beyond tests.
- T1: pilot, 20 pairs, 4 seeds, per-regime verdict: oblivious, oracle-time, oracle-target, oracle-keep, window, periodic, stationary controls (for c_FA). About 20 minutes. Stop if N0 fails in the gated regime.
- T2: detector tuning (disjoint pairs), then test pairs: CUSUM and controls, SP-stale. About 100 pairs x 4 seeds.
- T3: conditional on TH-N3; own plan.
- Noise 5 % as a robustness arm after N1.

## 6. Risks
- Stylised change (task switch on a shared grid): resembles a new raw-material batch or recalibration, not gradual drift; partial drift is the nearest proxy. State in the write-up.
- On related pairs stale data may still help and prediction errors barely move at T_c; the regime-conditional gates mean N0 and N1 are not required there.
- Detector tuning can overfit its pairs; tuning and test pairs are disjoint.
- Late changes leave few post-change steps; reported by T_c.
- A narrow gated regime reduces the claim to "restart pays when tasks differ".

## 7. What the result would add to the thesis
N0 fail: stationary conclusions extend; no regime found where reactivity pays. N0 pass, N1 fail: change matters but is not detectable from the campaign's own data. N1 pass: reactivity pays only under non-stationarity, and the controller is numeric. N3 pass: the first case where a typed model has a decision role.

**Pairs and seeds.** Detector and parameter tuning on the 40 tuning pairs (seeds 4900-4903). Confirmatory gates on the 100 untouched test pairs (seeds 5000-5003); T_c in {25 %, 50 %}; a in {1, 0.5}. The gated regime stays unrelated x abrupt.

**New arms** (restart arms oracle_time / oracle_target stay as references; periodic and CUSUM-restart are dropped):
| Arm | Definition |
|---|---|
| oblivious | as before |
| refresh | **oblivious + incumbent re-measurement, no response**: every r BO steps one step re-evaluates the current incumbent and replaces its stored value (fixes the stale-recommendation confound; the baseline all gains are measured against) |
| remeasure_keep | as refresh; if the re-measured value differs from the stored one by more than tol, mark all stored data stale (variance 0.1, re-evaluable) and continue. tol = 1e-9 at noise 0; under noise a pre-declared band (3 x sqrt(2) x noise sd) |
| oracle_keep | as before (knows T_c; keeps stale data with inflated noise) |
| cusum_keep | CUSUM on GP prediction error (as before) with the keep response instead of restart; thresholds tuned on the tuning pairs |
| sp_passive | **no detector**: from the start, observations older than the last 15 form a "history" source row (GP mean over the grid), added to the real sources; weights (kappa 10, lambda .25, c .25) and the surrogate use only the last 15 observations. If nothing changes the history row agrees and costs little; after a change it disagrees and its weight collapses |
| sp_stale_remeasure | SP-stale (stale row = GP on pre-detection data) triggered by the re-measurement |
| oracle_time, oracle_target | reference upper bounds (restart) |
Forced-event stationary controls for cost: forced_keep (mark everything stale at 50 %), forced_spstale (SP-stale event at 50 %).

**Re-measurement parameters.** r in {5, 10, 20} chosen on the tuning pairs by a rule fixed now: among r values whose stationary harm vs oblivious is <= 2 %, the one with the lowest mean post-change AUC ratio vs refresh; if none qualify, the smallest harm, flagged. Each re-measurement uses one budget step (counted in all AUCs). At noise 0 the re-measurement false-alarm rate is exactly 0; this is checked empirically on the stationary runs, not assumed.

**Gates (confirmatory, test pairs only; ratio of means over pairs, one-sided Wilcoxon, Holm over A1-A4; baseline = refresh unless stated).**
- **TH-A0 (headroom beyond the fixable artefact):** oracle-keep at least 10 % below refresh in post-change AUC, unrelated x abrupt, p < 0.05, same sign in both strata. If not, the pilot headroom was the recommendation rule and detection has nothing left to win: report and stop.
- **Cost rule (pre-committed, same formula as section 4):** c_FA measured for forced_keep and forced_spstale on the tuning pairs; FA budget = min(10 %, 0.02 / c_FA). Used only to set cusum_keep's threshold; re-measurement needs none. If the budget is below 2 % for the soft response too, cusum_keep is declared infeasible and not run on the test pairs.
- **TH-A1 (active detection):** the best of {remeasure_keep, sp_stale_remeasure, cusum_keep} (chosen on tuning pairs) at least 5 % below refresh in the gated regime, and not more than 2 % worse than refresh on stationary runs and in the related regimes (no-harm condition, reported per regime). Report share of oracle-keep's gain recovered, detection delay and delay-window regret.
- **TH-A2 (passive suffices?):** sp_passive stationary harm vs oblivious <= 2 %, and sp_passive within 2 % of or better than the best active detector in the gated regime. Pass = "a soft transfer layer needs no detector".
- **TH-A3 (active beats passive):** the best active detector at least 2 % below sp_passive with p < 0.05 in the gated regime and no harm in related regimes. A1 pass + A3 pass = the first regime in this project where active reactivity demonstrably pays; A2 pass = detection is unnecessary there. Either, both-fail, or neither is reportable.
- Cheap detector first: if remeasure_keep passes A1 and nothing beats it, the controller is a one-line rule and no model is needed. The typed model (section 4, TH-N3) stays struck unless a response headroom exists that a numeric rule on the same state cannot reach.

**Stages (stop and ask at each).**
- A0 (build, tests, no compute beyond tests): `refresh`, `remeasure_keep`, `cusum_keep`, `sp_passive`, `sp_stale_remeasure`, forced events in `meta/nonstat.py`. Tests: re-measure consumes one step and replaces the stored value; mismatch triggers at noise 0 only after a change; zero triggers on stationary runs; refresh equals oblivious if r is larger than the budget; sp_passive uses only the last 15 observations for the surrogate, history row built from older observations only, equals standard SP-like behaviour when the history is empty; keep-mode marks data stale and keeps it re-evaluable; determinism, budget.
- A1 (tuning pairs, about 1 h): choose r, measure c_FA for the soft responses, tune cusum_keep threshold, stationary harm of sp_passive. Stop if nothing is feasible.
- A2 (test pairs, about 3.5 h on 12 workers by the pilot's 6 s per campaign): all arms, A0 to A3, plus stationary controls. One analysis pass, no gate edits afterwards.
- A3 (optional, own approval): 5 % observation noise, which requires the tolerance band above.

**Caveats written in advance.** Part of any detection gain on TabRepo comes from anti-correlated pairs, where stale data actively misleads. The full-grid Spearman pairing says little about agreement near the optimum. The pilot's soft-vs-restart ordering rests on 10 pairs per regime. sp_passive is a new design (window + history row) whose choices (15, history definition) are fixed here without tuning and could be wrong; a failure of TH-A2 would not show that passive handling cannot work.

### 9b. Pins added before A0 (review of Amendment 1; these are what the A0 tests encode)
- **P1 controls with the keep response (re-added).** `random_keep_rm`: same re-measurement schedule as remeasure_keep, but the keep trigger fires at random on non-re-measurement steps at a fixed per-step rate (rate = remeasure_keep's mean trigger count per BO step on the tuning changed campaigns, set once). `random_keep`: no re-measurement, random trigger at cusum_keep's matched rate, keep response. `shuffled_keep`: cusum_keep fed z values bootstrapped causally from its own past. A1 additionally requires the frozen detector to beat its matching random control by at least 2 % (post-change AUC, gated regime).
- **P2 history row.** Posterior mean (ARD-RBF GP via `bo._gp`) of the observations older than the last 15, evaluated on the full grid; built only when at least 3 such observations exist (otherwise sp_passive equals the plain surrogate on all data). Same function as `stale_row`. sp_passive recommends the evaluated config with the highest fused posterior mean (window + history + sources), because "best stored value among the last 15" would forget an old incumbent in stationary runs; this recommendation rule belongs to the arm's design and is flagged as another untuned choice. Older configs re-enter the candidate set (as in the window arm); a re-evaluation replaces the old stored entry of that config.
- **P3 detector selection.** Tune-select-freeze: on the 40 tuning pairs, the detector among {remeasure_keep, sp_stale_remeasure, cusum_keep} with the lowest pooled post-change AUC ratio vs refresh over the four regimes (equal weights) is frozen; only that detector is gated on the test pairs, the others are secondary. Thresholds are tuned on the tuning pairs only.
- **P4 mechanics.** Keep response: on trigger every stored observation (except a just-refreshed incumbent) gets fixed observation variance 0.1 (standardised units), fresh observations 1e-4; the marking is permanent (a stale point regains full trust only by being re-evaluated, which replaces it); at most **one** keep event per campaign (single-change simulator; a second trigger is ignored). r rule: one r in {5, 10, 20}, shared by refresh, remeasure_keep, random_keep_rm and sp_stale_remeasure, chosen on the tuning pairs by J(r) = (pooled post-change AUC ratio of remeasure_keep vs oblivious over the four regimes - 1) + (stationary AUC ratio of remeasure_keep vs oblivious - 1), the smallest J wins (change and no-change scenarios weighted equally). This replaces the "stationary harm <= 2 %" wording of section 9, which would almost surely pick r = 20 by construction. Re-measurement steps count against the budget; the same formula `(t + 1) % r == 0` and the same RNG streams are used by all re-measuring arms so they are paired until their first trigger.
- **Decision rule A2/A3 (one rule).** The confirmatory claim is A3 (active beats passive by >= 2 %, paired Wilcoxon p < 0.05). If A3 fails, the fallback reading "passive suffices" requires a bootstrap 90 % CI over pairs of (active / sp_passive - 1) inside [-2 %, +2 %] and sp_passive's stationary harm <= 2 % vs oblivious; otherwise "inconclusive". Every A2 statement carries the qualifier that it concerns this sp_passive design.
- **Expected costs, stated in advance.** refresh (and every re-measuring arm) loses to oblivious on stationary runs by roughly the re-measurement budget share (about 1/r of the steps) at noise 0, because re-measurement is pure waste there. Gains are reported against both oblivious (continuity with the pilot) and refresh (the deployable baseline). Because the anti-correlated unrelated pairs are 22/23 TabRepo, the split cannot support its own gate; the gate uses all 50 unrelated pairs.
- **Staging of A2.** Active arms and controls first, then the passive and reference arms, so a failure at A1 or in the active arms can stop the run. The 5 % noise arm (tolerance band, real false-alarm rate) is a conditional stage after A1.

### 9c. A1 pins (declared before any A1 data)
- **Keep variance.** 0.1 / 1e-4 are in *standardised-observation units* (observations are standardised over the active data inside the fixed-noise GP). Decision (b): sensitivity v in {0.01, 0.1, 0.3} on the tuning pairs, frozen by v* = argmin_v J_v, J_v = [mean over the four regimes of (post-change AUC ratio of means, oracle_keep(v) vs oblivious) - 1] + [stationary AUC ratio of means, forced_keep(v) vs oblivious - 1]. Ties go to 0.1. Chosen before r (r is then chosen with remeasure_keep at v*).
- **CUSUM.** One tuned parameter, the threshold h in {2, 4, 8, 16, 32}. Fixed, untuned: reference value k = 1.0 on |z| (E|z| is about 0.8 under a calibrated model), z = (y_new - mu) / sd from the surrogate used for the proposal, reset after a trigger, one keep event per campaign. Criterion: among h whose stationary false-alarm rate (share of stationary campaigns with a trigger) is at most the FA budget min(10 %, 0.02 / c_FA(keep)), c_FA(keep) = relative stationary AUC increase of forced_keep(v*) vs oblivious, take the h with the lowest pooled post-change AUC ratio vs refresh(r*). If no h meets the budget, cusum_keep is infeasible and is not run on test pairs.
- **Random-control rate.** Per-campaign trigger probability q of the frozen detector = 0.5 x (share of tuning *changed* campaigns with a trigger) + 0.5 x (share of tuning *stationary* campaigns with a trigger); per-step rate = q / 50 (B = 50 for all landscapes). random_keep_rm uses remeasure_keep's q, random_keep uses cusum_keep's q. Carried unchanged to the test pairs.
- **A1 internal stop rules** (any one stops A1 and is reported as the result): (a) refresh's pooled post-change AUC ratio vs oblivious is above 1 for every r; (b) the frozen detector does not beat its matching random control by at least 2 % (pooled post-change AUC) on the tuning pairs; (c) oracle_keep(v*) is less than 5 % below refresh(r*) in the gated regime (unrelated x abrupt) on the tuning pairs (an early version of TH-A0). Flagged, not stopping: sp_passive stationary harm vs oblivious above 5 %.
- **Reports in A1.** Per-regime trigger rates and delays (including pre-T_c triggers, which spend the single keep event), the three FA costs (forced_keep, forced_spstale, refresh's re-measurement share), the r and v tables with J, sp_passive's stationary harm.
- **Staging and cost.** S1a (v), S1b (r), S2 (c_FA(spstale), CUSUM h), S3 (sp_stale_remeasure, sp_passive, shuffled_keep, random controls, selection). About 2.5 to 3 h in total on 12 workers (6 s per campaign), not 1 h. Seeds 4900-4903, T_c in {25 %, 50 %}, a in {1, 0.5}, the 40 tuning pairs.
- **Freeze manifest.** After A1: {r*, v*, detector identity, h* (if cusum_keep), q for the random controls}, written to this file as section 9d before any test-pair compute.

## 9e. Amendment 2 (2026-11-01, after A1 and before any test-pair compute; POST-HOC with respect to the sp_passive pin, flagged)

**Why.** A1 showed sp_passive at +140 % stationary harm. The likely cause is the design confound, not the passive idea: the last-15 window discards most of the campaign on every stationary run and a 15-point ARD residual is poorly identified. sp_passive therefore does not test the hypothesis stated earlier (all observations stay, stale data is one more source row, Spearman weights collapse it when it disagrees). That arm did not exist.

**New arm `sp_stale_full`** (implemented, tests in `meta/test_nonstat_a2.py`, 7 pass): no window, no detector, no trigger, no restart. The surrogate residual and the Spearman weights use **every** observation; the history row (GP mean of observations older than the last 15, built once at least 3 exist, same function as `stale_row`) is one extra source row; the recommendation is the evaluated config with the highest fused posterior mean. Same kappa 10, lambda .25, c .25. Until the history has 3 points it equals the plain engine. It is **untuned**; the 15 and the history definition are not varied. Nothing about it was tuned on the tuning pairs, and none of its parameters are tuned on test.

**Decision rule.** sp_passive stays in for continuity. Both passive arms are reported against the frozen detector (remeasure_keep). A3 (the pre-declared confirmatory claim) is evaluated against the **better** of the two passive arms (lower pooled post-change AUC in the gated regime), with Holm over the tests as pinned in 9b; a comparison against each is also shown. "Passive suffices" needs the 90 % bootstrap CI of (active / passive - 1) inside +-2 % and that passive arm's stationary harm <= 2 % vs oblivious. A passive failure only lowers the prior of the passive idea; it does not close it. Every statement is qualified as concerning these two untuned designs.

## 9f. Pre-registration of the noise stage (A3-noise; written 2026-11-01, before A2 completes; run only on a separate approval)

Reason: the zero-false-alarm property of remeasure_keep is a noise-0 property. With observation noise the incumbent re-measures differently and, because the incumbent is the maximum of noisy values, it regresses toward the mean even without any change.
- Noise: Gaussian observation noise with sd = 5 % of the landscape's (y_max - y_median) spread in score units, using the definition of the earlier 5 % arms (to be read from that code and quoted, not redefined). The new observation noise applies to all evaluations including re-measurements; regret is always scored on the noiseless value of the recommended config.
- Tolerance band: a mismatch is declared when |re-measured - stored| > m x sqrt(2) x sigma, with m in {1, 2, 3, 4}. **Only m is tuned**, on the 40 tuning pairs with fresh seeds 5100-5103, under noise. v* and r* are the noise-0 values (not re-tuned). Recommendation uses the stored value of the incumbent (the last measurement), unchanged.
- Selection rule (same J-rule and freeze discipline as A1): among m whose stationary false-alarm rate (share of stationary campaigns with a keep trigger) is at most min(10 %, 0.02 / c_FA(keep, noise)), where c_FA(keep, noise) is re-measured with forced_keep under noise on the tuning pairs, take the m with the lowest pooled post-change AUC ratio vs refresh. If no m meets the budget, the detector is declared infeasible under noise; that is the result.
- Controls: refresh, random_keep_rm at the tuning trigger rate (q rule of 9c), oracle_keep, oblivious, sp_stale_full. Gates on the 100 test pairs with fresh seeds 5200-5203: TH-A1 and TH-A3 as before, plus the **empirical** stationary false-alarm rate reported with a Wilson 95 % interval.
- Freeze manifest for m* written to this file (section 9g) before any noise test compute. The A2 outcome cannot change any of the above except the decision whether to run it.

## 9g. Pre-declared diagnostic: why CUSUM failed (written before the diagnostic is run)

Hypotheses. H_explore: prediction surprise is driven by exploration, i.e. |z| is large when LogEI moves to a configuration far from every previous observation (large distance in X to the nearest earlier observation, large posterior sd), so the stream looks like a change point without one. H_miscal: z is simply mis-scaled (median |z| well above the 0.8 expected under calibration even at exploitative steps, i.e. close to earlier observations). Data: a re-run of cusum_keep (h = 32, v*) on the 40 tuning pairs, seed 4900, stationary and change (T_c 50 %, abrupt), logging per step (|z|, distance of the proposal to the nearest earlier observation, posterior sd, re-evaluation flag). Statistics: Spearman(|z|, distance) and Spearman(|z|, sd) within campaign then averaged over campaigns; median |z| for the lowest and highest distance tertile; share of stationary CUSUM crossings that occur at a step in the top distance tertile; |z| at T_c to T_c + 3 vs other steps on change runs. Reading rule: H_explore is supported if the mean Spearman(|z|, distance) >= 0.3 and the top-tertile median |z| is at least 1.5 x the bottom-tertile median; H_miscal if the bottom-tertile median |z| exceeds 1.2; both can hold. Report whichever comes out.

## 9i. Noise stage, final pre-registration (2026-11-01; supersedes the tuning scope of 9f; approved with re-tuning pins; written before any noise compute)

**Result recorded first (trace check, `meta/diag_missed.py`, `meta/results/diag_missed.txt`).** The statement "remeasure_keep misses only if y_A(x*) = y_B(x*)" is **false as stated**: of 263 never-triggered campaigns (of 1600 on the test pairs) 47 had identical incumbent values under both objectives, 3 were mixed, and 213 had a differing incumbent whose *stored value was already post-change* (the incumbent was observed after T_c, so there was nothing stale to detect). The substantive claim holds in a weaker form: on the missed campaigns refresh already has a low post-change AUC (0.084 vs oblivious 0.130) and oracle_keep is worse than refresh (+49.8 %); a perfect keep/no-keep chooser on the missed campaigns would remove only **0.9 %** of remeasure_keep's total post-change AUC mass (56 of 263 missed campaigns have an oracle_keep advantage above 0.01 AUC). So the residual gap for a smarter detector is under 1 % in this regime at noise 0. (This check is post-hoc, requested after A2; the numbers come from stored A2 runs plus a rerun of the missed campaigns.)

**Noise definition (from `meta/bo.py` `run`).** sd = noise_frac x (y_max - y_median) of the objective, noise_frac = 0.05. The earlier arms drew **one fixed noise value per grid point**, which would make a re-measurement return the identical value. **Declared deviation:** this stage draws an independent N(0, sd^2) value for every evaluation (initial design, BO steps, restart designs, re-measurements); sd uses the pre-change objective's span (= 1 in score units) before T_c and the post-change objective's own (y_max - y_median) after. Regret is always scored on the noiseless true value of the recommendation, which is chosen by the best stored (noisy) value as before. The fixed-noise GP of the keep path uses fresh variance max(1e-4, (sd / std of stored values)^2) and stale variance = fresh + v.

**Detector.** Mismatch when |re-measured - stored| > delta = m x sqrt(2) x sd_pre (sd known; in practice estimated from replicates). The incumbent's regression to the mean (it is the argmax of noisy values) biases the difference upward, so false alarms are expected to exceed the nominal Gaussian rate.

**Everything the noise touches is re-tuned on the 40 tuning pairs, seeds 5100-5103, under noise, T_c in {25 %, 50 %}, a in {1, .5}; same J rules, same freeze discipline, manifest in section 9j before any noise test compute.**
1. v in {0.01, 0.1, 0.3}: v* = argmin J_v, J_v = [pooled post-change ratio oracle_keep(v)/oblivious - 1] + [stationary ratio forced_keep(v)/oblivious - 1] (ties to 0.1), as in 9c.
2. c_FA(keep, noise) = stationary AUC increase of forced_keep(v*) vs oblivious; FA budget = min(10 %, 0.02 / c_FA).
3. Joint (r, m), r in {5, 10, 20}, m in {1, 2, 3, 4}, with remeasure_keep at v*: J(r, m) = [pooled post-change ratio remeasure_keep/oblivious - 1] + [stationary ratio remeasure_keep/oblivious - 1], restricted to cells whose stationary false-alarm rate (share of stationary campaigns with a keep trigger) is at most the FA budget; smallest J wins. This J now trades tolerance, false alarms (cost via the stationary term), delay and misses (post-change term), and the stationary term includes the re-measurement budget cost.
4. Matching random control (random_keep_rm, rate = q / 50, q by the 9c rule from the chosen cell), refresh at r*.
- **Expected stationary false-alarm range, stated before the data** (4 to 5 re-measurements per campaign; difference sd = sqrt(2) x sd; incumbent winner's-curse bias roughly 1 to 2 sd): m = 1: 70-100 %; m = 2: 25-70 %; m = 3: 5-25 %; m = 4: 1-10 %. Expected feasible cells (FA <= 10 %): m = 3 or 4, with m = 4 the safest. If m = 4 also exceeds the budget the detector is infeasible at 5 % noise, and that is the result, not a tuning failure. Expected post-change cost: detection delay grows and the miss rate rises (changes at the incumbent smaller than delta = 0.21 x (m / 3) go undetected).
- **Stop rules (tuning):** (a) refresh harmful vs oblivious for every r; (b) frozen detector not at least 2 % better than its matching random control; (c) oracle_keep(v*) less than 5 % below refresh(r*) in the gated regime; (d) no (r, m) cell within the FA budget (infeasible: stop and report). Flagged, not stopping: remeasure_keep stationary harm vs refresh above 2 %.
- **Gates on the 100 test pairs (seeds 5200-5203), identical to the noise-free ones, line by line:** TH-A0 (oracle_keep >= 10 % below refresh, gated, both strata), TH-A1 (remeasure_keep >= 5 % below refresh gated, no harm > 2 % vs refresh on stationary runs and in related regimes, >= 2 % better than the matched random control, share of oracle_keep's gain recovered reported), TH-A3 (vs the better passive arm, >= 2 %, p < 0.05; and the harm-bound reading of the passive arms). Added reports: the empirical stationary false-alarm rate with a Wilson 95 % interval, misses, delay, gain given trigger.
- **Replicate effect (exploratory, flagged):** `refresh_rep` keeps each re-measurement as an extra replicate row in the GP (so the surrogate can estimate the noise) instead of replacing the stored value. Reported on stationary and change runs against refresh at r*, to measure whether replicates offset part of refresh's stationary cost. Not part of any gate.
- Untuned passive arms on test only: sp_passive, sp_stale_full (unchanged designs).

## 9k. Scope statements to carry into the write-up (not new tests)
- **CUSUM.** The pre-registered result is that off-the-shelf CUSUM on raw standardised GP prediction errors (k = 1.0, h in {2..32}, one keep event) is infeasible under the false-alarm budget. The post-hoc diagnostic (tuning pairs, seed 4900) rejected both pre-declared mechanisms (exploration: Spearman(|z|, distance) = -0.07; mis-scaling: low-distance median |z| = 0.67) and shows a heavy-tailed error stream (median |z| 0.73, mean 1.89) in which the change is visible (|z| 5.69 at T_c..T_c+3 vs 2.18). Winsorised or rank-based variants might pass; none was run, and the infeasibility claim is not extended to them (future work).
- **A3 comparator.** The comparator (sp_passive as the "better passive arm") was selected by gated test-data AUC and is post-hoc. The conclusion does not depend on it: both passive arms fail the stationary-harm bound by an order of magnitude (+172.5 %, +14.0 % vs <= 2 %), so "passive suffices" fails regardless. Lead with the harm bound; then the head-to-head (-19.4 %, p = 0.089, Holm 0.178, 90 % CI [-34.1, -0.4]), a power-limited effect, not an absent one. Both passive designs are untuned. Neither implements the shrinkage mechanism w = (1 - lambda) x uniform + lambda x softmax in a way that lets the history row's weight collapse to uniform on its own; the SP safety argument was never instantiated here, which is the most likely route to fixing the +14 % harm (future work).
- **Keep-gain decomposition table** (from A2, gated unrelated x abrupt, post-change AUC vs refresh): response alone (random_keep_rm) -13.1 %; response + timing (remeasure_keep) -39.7 %; oracle ceiling (oracle_keep) -55.6 %. Hence response about 13 points, timing precision about 27 points, unrecovered about 16 points (other regimes: random -9 to -18 %, remeasure -38 to -58 %, oracle -50 to -71 %). With v* = 0.01 (near-discard) the hedge sits in the trigger's precision, not the response's softness.
